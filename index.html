How these numbers are calculated
  • Migraine days – days with at least one migraine episode (two episodes on one day count as one day). Days you marked “No migraine” are shown separately from days with nothing recorded.
  • Planned workouts – completed planned sessions ÷ all planned sessions on your schedule for those days (using the schedule that applied then). Unplanned workouts are not part of the percentage; they are listed separately. Sessions you haven’t marked count as planned but not completed, and are shown as “not recorded” so you can see when tracking is incomplete.
  • Hard nights – nights where falling back asleep after the longest awakening took 30–60 min, over 60 min, or you couldn’t fall back asleep. Nights with no awakenings, or a quicker return to sleep, count as fine. Nights without that answer are “no data”.
  • Supplements & meds – taken ÷ (taken + missed) for scheduled doses. Doses not marked either way are shown separately and are not counted. As-needed medications are never included.
  • As-needed medications – every intake you logged. “Did it help?” comes from the migraine episode an intake is linked to.
  • Partial periods (before Oct 1, 2026 or after today) only count the days inside the range. These are simple counts and don’t show whether one thing causes another.
